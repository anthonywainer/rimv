## Consent choices — ask separately

- [ ] Participate in the study.
- [ ] Permit anonymized observation notes.
- [ ] Permit audio recording **of the research session**.
- [ ] Permit screen/video recording **of the research session**.
- [ ] Permit use of de-identified short quotations in internal reporting.
- [ ] Permit approved external sharing, if any, described specifically.

A refusal to research recording should not automatically prevent participation when non-recorded observation is practical. Explain technical requirements if recording is essential to a specific study.

## RimV-specific safeguards

- [ ] No real sensitive transcript or audio is required.
- [ ] Sample audio and test accounts are synthetic/sanitized.
- [ ] On-screen notifications, files and identifiers are sanitized.
- [ ] Product microphone permission is not treated as research consent.
- [ ] Withdrawal and deletion handling is clear.
- [ ] Raw data storage is approved and access restricted.
- [ ] Study participants are represented as `P01`, `P02`, etc. in shared findings.
